Clinical Background & Reality

The invisible gap in prosthetic rehabilitation

During the ambulatory phase, the adaptation process loses continuity. The absence of objective gait data in everyday environments leads to preventable secondary complications.

01 // OUTPATIENT ISOLATION

Loss of clinical visibility outside the clinic

Specialists lack visibility into how patients walk at home. Without timely feedback, prescribed therapies are abandoned or performed incorrectly.

73% abandonment or incorrect exercise execution
02 // ASSOCIATED PATHOLOGIES

Postural anomalies and asymmetric loading

Undetected compensatory movements cause weight imbalances that lead to chronic lower back pain or residual limb discomfort.

68% develop chronic lower back pain from compensation
03 // INTER-INSTITUTIONAL DISCONNECT

Fragmented multidisciplinary care team

Communication between patients, rehabilitation clinics, and orthopedic centers is reactive and disjointed, hindering timely adjustments.

Delayed prosthetic adjustments & fragmented medical history

CLINICAL INQUIRIES & SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about sensor compatibility, medical data privacy, and clinical deployment.

Prothia uses lightweight, wireless low-power sensors that comfortably attach to any type of prosthesis or limb. They do not interfere with movement or add perceptible weight, allowing completely natural walking.

Yes. As an open-standard, multi-brand platform, our sensors pair seamlessly with passive mechanical prostheses as well as myoelectric and bionic components from leading manufacturers (such as Ottobock or Össur), adapting to the patient's kinematic protocol.

We strictly adhere to international health confidentiality standards (AES-256 encryption in transit and at rest, and HIPAA/GDPR-compliant guidelines). The platform enforces strict role-based access control (RBAC); patients always retain ownership over which specialists or institutions can access their records.

Deployment is immediate. Prothia runs as a cloud-based web application requiring no on-premise servers. We provide a 90-minute virtual onboarding session to train physiatrists and therapists on creating patient cohorts and interpreting telemetric dashboards.

Sensors and the web application are engineered with an offline-first architecture. All gait data and exercise sessions are stored securely in local device memory and automatically synchronize with the medical cloud as soon as a Wi-Fi or cellular connection is re-established.
